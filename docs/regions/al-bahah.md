## English brief

**Al-Bahah Region** is a mountainous province in southwestern Saudi Arabia, immediately north of Asir. It is home to the Azd Qahtanite tribes of **Ghamid** and **Zahran**, as well as eastern branches of **Banu Shahr (Shahran)**. The capital is the city of Al-Bahah. The region is famous for the **Raghadan juniper forest** (largest in the Arabian Peninsula), historic stone forts in Baljurashi, and a mild summer climate that attracts tourists from across the Kingdom. Population ~480,000; 12,300 km².
